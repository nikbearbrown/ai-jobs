# SEIS-fit postings in the 2026-10-27 collection

## Executive summary

**What this is.** The 82,650 open postings read from 715 confirmed company boards on 2026-10-27, sorted by whether the *title* looks like work a SEIS graduate is hired into.
**What was found.** 24,576 of 59,450 postings (41%) have a title in a SEIS title family (counted without Domino's, whose 23,200 store-level postings would otherwise be 28% of everything). **1,939 are marked entry-level or internship by their title, 1,112 of them in the United States** (list in `entry-level.csv`).
**How far to trust it.** Title only. A title with no level word says nothing about experience required, so 'entry-level' undercounts what a new graduate could actually apply for and the bulk of the roles are simply unlabelled. Location is read from the text the board gave (US states or 'United States'), not geocoded. Only companies on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters and Recruitee are here; most banks, hospitals, consultancies and government sit on systems not yet read (see `data/companies/ats-coverage.md`).
