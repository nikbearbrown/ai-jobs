# SEIS-fit postings in the 2026-11-24 collection

## Executive summary

**What this is.** The 84,517 open postings read from 716 confirmed company boards on 2026-11-24, sorted by whether the *title* looks like work a SEIS graduate is hired into.
**What was found.** 24,208 of 59,356 postings (40%) have a title in a SEIS title family (counted without Domino's, whose 25,161 store-level postings would otherwise be 29% of everything). **1,979 are marked entry-level or internship by their title, 1,138 of them in the United States** (list in `entry-level.csv`).
**How far to trust it.** Title only. A title with no level word says nothing about experience required, so 'entry-level' undercounts what a new graduate could actually apply for and the bulk of the roles are simply unlabelled. Location is read from the text the board gave (US states or 'United States'), not geocoded. Only companies on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters and Recruitee are here; most banks, hospitals, consultancies and government sit on systems not yet read (see `data/companies/ats-coverage.md`).
